# Ascendant: a fourth faction (proof of concept)

A gameplay mod that adds a faction to the lobby's faction dropdown, with two
commanders to choose from. It has no C#: the faction is declared in
`mod.json`, and the units are Lua templates. You pick Ascendant (or
"Ascendant (Warden)"), spawn its commander, and the commander builds an
Ascendant land factory, which builds Ascendant tanks and raiders. All of it
uses EDA's models.

## What's in it

- `mod.json`: the faction under `factions`. It has the name, the `ua` unit-id
  prefix, the `ASCENDANT` tag, the icon, two commanders, and `"looksLike":
  "EDA"` so shields and build beams use EDA's materials. The Mod API does
  everything else:
  - the dropdown entries and their icon
  - the faction's number and its `FactionsData` entries
  - moving seats when the mod is dropped
  - letting the stock AI play it
- `icons\ascendant.png`: the dropdown icon.
- `lua\common\units\unitsTemplates\*`: the units, generated by
  `tools\make-templates.ps1` from the game's EDA templates. Each borrows its
  EDA model with `general.modelTpId`. The framework makes the placement
  ghost, portrait and wreck follow.

## Try it

Copy the folder (`mod.json`, `icons\` and `lua\`) into
`engine\SanctuaryMods\AscendantFaction`. In a lobby, the host picks the mod
from the Mods panel. Anyone can then choose Ascendant as their faction, and
the host can give it to an AI.

After a game update, run `pwsh -File tools/make-templates.ps1` to regenerate the
units from the current EDA ones.

## Known limits

- **Art:** every unit borrows EDA's. A faction with its own art ships it in
  `packs\` (see "Art packs" in the guide).
- **AI:** the stock AI plays Ascendant, but the roster is small: land only,
  tech 1, one factory type.
- **Vanilla players:** someone without the mod who is in the lobby when a
  player picks Ascendant sees a faction their game doesn't know. Start is
  blocked for them anyway, since they lack the mod.
- **Repeat build:** the factory inherits EDA's repeat-build default.
