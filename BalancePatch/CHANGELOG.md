# Balance Patch 0.2.8

Against game 0.0.1.20 (Steam build 25474094). Each change is the game's number,
then this release's. Factions share a line where their change is the same. Every section is a lobby
option, on by default unless it is listed under Optional. Generated by `node BalancePatch/tools/preview.mjs --changelog`.

## Sections

- **Fixes**: Bugs: a bomber with no muzzles stopped other units' targeting; aircraft inside a shield's bubble hit everything under it; the Chosen T2 point defence and the EDA T3 anti-air fighter shot the ground; bombs expired before they could land; the TALEN gunship was labelled tier 1.
- **Economy**: Commanders make 3 alloys and 30 energy a second (were 5 and 50) and start you with full storage, 500 alloys and 5000 energy (was half, 250 and 2500). T2 and T3 extractors make 5 and 15 (were 4 and 10); generators make 20/600/2500 energy (were 10/200/1000), and T3 ones cost 2800 alloys (were 5000). Factories cost 200 alloys (were 150), upgrading one to T2 costs 1000 (was 500) over 90 s (was 80) and to T3 4000 (was 2000), and T3 tanks and the Guardian T3 raider cost 30% less. T2 radar uses 250 energy a second (was 150) and T3 radar costs 2800 alloys (was 700) and 1400 energy a second (was 350); EDA and Chosen T3 radar see 750 and 850 (were 450 and 550). Wrecks reclaim 3x as fast and last 6 minutes (were 3). Land units cost 6 energy per alloy (were 10), Chosen aircraft as much energy as the others, and engineers have less than half their health.
- **Commanders**: The EDA and Guardian commanders' missiles fly at once and steer at where the target is going, in smaller, faster volleys: they hit moving units as often as the Chosen commander does.
- **Artillery**: Artillery leads moving targets (it couldn't hit anything moving in a straight line); the three factions' T1 artillery are brought level.
- **Air**: Bombers lead their targets and their bombs splash; anti-air leads its shots and the T3 anti-air towers reach their range; the Guardian fighters catch up with the others. Anti-air hits most of the time: faster shells and missiles, no drop or spread, beams aim where the plane is, and aircraft hitboxes 1.5x bigger. The Guardian T2 anti-air (big splash) does half damage.
- **Defences**: Point defences see as far as they shoot, and T1 point defences are tougher: they beat their cost in T1 tanks, while T1 artillery still outranges them. T2 point defences are much tougher and hit harder, and the Chosen T3 one hits much harder.
- **Unit tuning**: The Chosen Jager toned down and the other T2 raiders brought up, so T2 beats its cost in T1 without crushing it; the three T1 tanks brought level; T3 tanks move as fast as T1 tanks with quicker guns, and the Guardian T3 raider is faster still and lighter; T4 bots move at 2.25-3 (were 2; none faster than T3 tanks) and are game enders: 10000-25000 alloys with health and damage to match, less for the ones whose splash and range already win fights; the EDA railgun sniper reaches 80.

## Rules

- **Targeting** (with Fixes on): a weapon whose muzzles aren't in its model (the Guardian T1 and T3 bombers, the Chosen T3 bomber, the EDA T2 raider, the TALEN) picks no target. Before, its target search threw an error that stopped every weapon queued after it from picking a new target, every tick, for as long as it had an enemy in range.
- **Aircraft inside shields** (with Fixes on): an aircraft's projectile that starts inside an enemy shield's bubble and lands inside it hits the shield instead, and so does an aircraft's beam fired from inside one. Before, gunships and bombers flew inside T3 bubbles and hit everything under them. Units on the ground inside a bubble shoot as before.
- **Start** (with Economy on): an army starts with its commander's storage full, 500 alloys and 5000 energy (the game gives half)
- **Reclaim** (with Economy on): wrecks reclaim 3x as fast (the game takes the unit's build time); a wreck still holds half the unit's alloys
- **Wrecks** (with Economy on): last 6 minutes before they disappear (were 3)

## AI

- **Stock AI**: builds generators to energy-to-alloy income targets x0.7 (20 -> 14 early; 13-15 -> 9.1-10.5 later), as land units cost 6 energy per alloy instead of 10 (with Economy on)

## Why

**Fixes**

- The EDA T3 anti-air fighter's second launcher fired at land and sea. It now fires at aircraft like the first, which also puts its anti-air damage level with the other factions' T3 fighters.
- Bombs lived 2 seconds at speed 20, about 40 range, while bombers release from up to 60 (T1) or 90 (T3): the EDA T3 bomber fired all the time and never hit anything. They now live long enough to land.
- Likewise for T1 bombs.
- The Chosen T2 point defence (Redoubt) shot the ground: its model's barrel bone rests about 55 degrees nose-down, and it may only pitch up 45, so it could never raise the barrel to level. It never came on target (0% in tests at 20-47 range, other T2 point defences 48-94%). It may pitch up 100 now, rests level, and turns the model's real yaw bone (it named Turret01, which the model doesn't have).
- The Guardian TALEN gunship is built by T3 air factories at T3 cost but was tagged and labelled tier 1.

**Economy**

- The commander makes 3 alloys and 30 energy a second (was 5 and 50): about three extractors' worth instead of five, so income comes from taking the map. Its storage stays 500 alloys and 5000 energy, but an army starts with it full (was half, 250 and 2500), so the opening isn't starved.
- T2 extractors make 5 alloys a second (were 4): the upgrade (600 alloys) pays back in 150 s instead of 200 s, so small maps can grow by upgrading once their spots are taken.
- T1 generators make 20 energy a second (were 10): power is half of what everything costs, and a generator now supports a factory and a bit more.
- T1 generators had 10x the health per energy of T2 and T3 ones.
- T2 generators give 1.5x the energy per cost of T1 ones (were equal): 600 a second.
- T3 generators make 2500 energy a second (were 1000) and cost 2800 alloys and 28000 energy (were 5000 and 50000; build time 2800, was 5000): about 2.25x the energy per cost of T1 ones (were equal), in a smaller step that feeds two or three Alloy Furnaces.
- T3 extractors make 15 alloys a second (were 10): the upgrade (2000 alloys) pays back in 200 s instead of 400 s, so T3 economy pays for itself inside a 20-30 minute game.
- T1 factories cost 200 alloys and 2000 energy (were 150 and 1500): a factory is a decision, not something to spam, without making the opening drag.
- Upgrading a factory to T2 costs 1000 alloys and 10000 energy (was 500 and 5000) and takes 90 s (was 80): about 11 alloys a second while it runs, so T2 has to be paid for without losing the game to T1 spam first.
- Upgrading a factory to T3 costs 4000 alloys and 40000 energy (was 2000 and 20000; same build time).
- T2 radar uses 250 energy a second to run (was 150).

**Commanders**

- EDA commander: half the damage per missile, twice as often (same damage per second), so less of it is wasted on targets that are already dead.
- Guardian commander: the same.
- The EDA and Guardian commanders' missile hung at speed 5 for about three seconds before flying, so anything moving walked out of its way (measured: 23-43% of its damage landed on moving T1 tanks, the Chosen commander's 72%). It now flies at once and steers at where its target is going.

**Artillery**

- Artillery aims where a moving target will be instead of where it was. Its spread still makes it miss sometimes.
- Guardian T1 artillery's single big shell lands most often once it leads (66% of its damage on crossing tanks, against Chosen's 31%), so less per shell.
- Chosen T1 artillery lost every fight against its own value in T1 tanks once the others were tuned: a little more splash.
- EDA T1 artillery's rocket salvo had almost no splash (0.5), so its spread shells mostly hit nothing (7% on crossing tanks).

**Air**

- Bombers aim where a moving target will be. Without it the Chosen T1 bomber landed 77% of its bombs on still tanks and none on moving ones.
- Anti-air weapons lead their targets. The T3 fighters, the T3 anti-air towers and others shot at where an aircraft was, so a moving plane was mostly missed: against weaving T1 bombers the T3 fighters went from 23-58% to 54-78%, the EDA T3 tower from 39% to 50%. Not the EDA T2 mobile anti-air (Komodo): leading, its shells fell from 71% to 1%. Not the beams (the EDA T2 mobile anti-air and T2 tower, the Guardian T1 tower and mobile anti-air): a beam hits at once, so aiming ahead of a crossing plane misses it (the EDA Komodo fell from 71% to 1% leading; 0.1.0-0.2.5 had the other three leading by mistake).
- The EDA T3 anti-air tower and the Behemoth's anti-air fired speed-10 shells that lived 5 seconds: 50 range out of 60. They now reach their full range.
- The Chosen T3 anti-air tower's missile turned at 10 degrees a second and lived 2 seconds (about 45 range out of 60), so it landed 9% on weaving T1 bombers. It turns like the EDA one (60) and reaches its range: 16-18%.
- Likewise for its missile's lifetime.
- The Guardian T3 anti-air tower aimed its slow, falling shells (speed 20) straight at its target and hit nothing: 0% on weaving T1 bombers, 3% on a hovering gunship. It fires faster shells and aims for their fall: 18% and 13%. Still the weakest T3 anti-air tower.
- T1 fighters' bullets lived exactly long enough to reach their range (0.5 s at speed 50 for 25), so anything pulling away outran them.
- The Guardian T1 fighter (Aerofoil) was the slowest fighter (12 against 15) and lost every fight with the other T1 fighters without killing any: as fast as them, and more damage.
- The Guardian T3 fighter (Contrail) lost to both other T3 fighters even leading its shots: more damage per shot.
- Bombs with no splash do nothing when they land beside a target: T1 bombs get some (the EDA one already had plenty).
- And T3 bombs a little more.
- Anti-air should hit most of the time against a plane that is being moved around, not miss most of it. Projectiles hit by a zero-width ray each tick against the aircraft's box, so a projectile's own size never counts; what decides it is how long the shot is in the air, whether it falls, and the size of the box. Anti-air shells and missiles leave 1.5x as fast (each weapon's aim speed; beams unchanged). In an offline model of every anti-air weapon against five aircraft (my model of the game's shot and hit code, not played), this with the changes below takes the average hit rate from 27% to about 62% against aircraft moved around every 1-2 s, and from 38% to 71% against ones flying straight.
- Anti-air missiles fly 1.5x as fast at most (EDA 30, was 20; Chosen 37.5, was 25) and turn twice as fast (120 degrees a second, was 60), so a dodging plane doesn't outturn them. The EDA T3 air scout shares the EDA missile.
- Anti-air shells fired straight (no arc) fell 1 unit per second squared and the aim doesn't allow for it, so slow shells passed under small aircraft. Shells only anti-air fires now fly straight (the Guardian T1 and T3 fighters share theirs with bombers and keep the fall).
- Aircraft hitboxes are 1.5x as big in every direction (a T1 bomber's was 1 x 0.5 x 1, a fraction of its model): the box is all a shot can hit.
- The slowest anti-air shells (T2 towers and mobile anti-air, the Chosen and Guardian T3 fighters, the Guardian T3 tower, the Chosen big bot) fly twice as fast again, and lose their spread (up to 3 degrees, 1-3 units off at range 50, wider than the hitbox): in the same model they went from 23-49% to 59-72% against moved aircraft.
- The Guardian T2 anti-air tower's shells (20 in the game) need twice as much again to reach most of its 50 range in time: 120 in all (12% to 69% in the model).
- The Guardian T2 anti-air tower and mobile anti-air do half damage: their shells splash 5 and 10 (the Chosen ones 2 and 3, the EDA ones are beams, which don't splash), so once anti-air started hitting most of the time (0.2.6) each hit took a chunk out of every aircraft in a group.

**Defences**

- T1 point defences saw 15 but shot 25, so T1 tanks (range 20) could kill one from outside its sight. They now see their full range: still more than a tank's, less than T1 artillery's 30.
- With nothing like walls to hide behind, a T1 point defence died to its own value in T1 tanks while killing half of them. More health makes it a real answer to T1 tanks and an early commander, while T1 artillery still outranges it.
- T2 point defences likewise (saw 25, shoot 50).
- T3 point defences likewise (saw 30, shoot 70).
- T2 point defences had the health of a T1 one at 2.5-3x the price: against their cost in T2 tanks they scored 0.18-0.28 (health x damage per alloy, range counted; my arithmetic), where FAF's T2 point defences score about 0.6 against FAF's T2 tanks. Health and damage raised together to about 0.65: they lose to their cost in T2 tanks that reach them, beat lower tiers, and still fall to artillery. The EDA one: 3000 health (was 2000), 1.52x damage.
- The Guardian T2 point defence: 3200 health (was 2000), 1.61x damage.
- The Chosen T2 point defence (the dearest, 572 alloys): 3800 health (was 2000), 1.9x damage.
- The Chosen T3 point defence scored 0.13 against its cost in T3 tanks (FAF's Ravager about 0.2). 7000 health (was 6500) and 2.24x damage (275 to about 615 a second): a hard-hitting but fragile turret, about 0.3.
