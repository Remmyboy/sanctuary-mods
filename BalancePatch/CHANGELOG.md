# Balance Patch 0.1.0

Every change against game 0.0.1.20 (Steam build 25474094). Each section
is a lobby option, on by default. Generated by `node BalancePatch/tools/preview.mjs --changelog`.

## Fixes

*The EDA T3 anti-air fighter's second launcher fired at land and sea. It now fires at aircraft like the first, which also puts its anti-air damage level with the other factions' T3 fighters.*

- **T3 Anti-Air Fighter** Peregrine (EDA): weapon 2 targets Land, WaterSurface -> Air, LandedAir

*Bombs lived 2 seconds at speed 20, about 40 range, while bombers release from up to 60 (T1) or 90 (T3): the EDA T3 bomber fired all the time and never hit anything. They now live long enough to land.*

- **T3 Bomber** Condor (EDA), Meteor (Chosen), Impulse (Guardian): projectile lifetime (s) 2 -> 5

*Likewise for T1 bombs.*

- **T1 Bomber** Comet (Chosen), Inertia (Guardian): projectile lifetime (s) 2 -> 4

*The Guardian TALEN gunship is built by T3 air factories at T3 cost but was tagged and labelled tier 1.*

- **T1 Gunship** TALEN (Guardian): name Tier 1: Gunship -> Tier 3: Gunship; tag TECH1 -> TECH3

## Economy

*Less commander income, so going straight to T2 off the commander alone takes longer and expanding matters more.*

- **Commander** (all factions): income 5 alloys/s + 50 energy/s -> 2 alloys/s + 30 energy/s

*T1 extractors make up the commander's lost alloy income, but only where you have taken the map.*

- **T1 Alloy Extractor** (all factions): alloys/s 1 -> 1.5

*T2 extractors pay back their upgrade faster than before (about 270 s against 400 s) instead of being the worse deal.*

- **T2 Alloy Extractor** (all factions): alloys/s 4 -> 6

*T3 extractors likewise (upgrade pays back in about 400 s, was 670 s).*

- **T3 Alloy Extractor** (all factions): alloys/s 10 -> 16

*T2 generators give 1.5x the energy per cost of T1 (were exactly equal).*

- **T2 Energy Generator** (all factions): energy/s 200 -> 300

*T3 generators give 2x the energy per cost of T1 (were exactly equal).*

- **T3 Energy Generator** (all factions): energy/s 1000 -> 2000

*T1 generators had 10x the health per energy of T2 and T3 ones.*

- **T1 Energy Generator** (all factions): health 800 -> 500
